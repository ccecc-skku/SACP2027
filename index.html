About the Conference

Across Asian and comparative philosophical traditions, embodiment has long been central to questions of self, ethics, community, and the human relationship to the world.

At a time of rapid technological change, ecological crisis, and global uncertainty, this conference invites renewed reflection on how texts, traditions, and lived experience shape agency, responsibility, and philosophical dialogue across cultures.

Graduate Student Essay Contest

SACP awards prizes for the top three papers presented by graduate students: First Place, Second Place, and Third Place.

Students must attend the conference and present their papers to be eligible. Those who wish their papers to be considered must submit both an abstract and a complete essay of no more than 5,000 words to SACP Secretary Kevin Taylor at SACPcontact@gmail.com by November 15, 2026.
